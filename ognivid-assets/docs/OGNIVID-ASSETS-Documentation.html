What is OGNIVID ASSETS?

OGNIVID ASSETS is the asset store of OGNIVID. It sells products that help people build WordPress websites faster with Elementor: complete page templates and plugins that plug straight into the Elementor editor.

Everything a customer buys lives in one place, their My Account dashboard: downloads, license keys, the websites using each license, and order history.

Who is it for?

Freelancers

Deliver client websites faster by starting from a finished, professional layout instead of a blank page.

Agencies

Standardise the look and quality of many client projects with one shared template library.

Business owners

Build or refresh your own company website without hiring a designer for every page.

WordPress learners

Study how real, well-structured Elementor pages are built, then adapt them.

FAQ

Do I need Elementor Pro?

No. The free version of Elementor is enough.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and the license never expires.

Can I move my license to another website?

Yes. Deactivate it on the old site from My Account, then activate it on the new one.

What happens to pages I already built if I deactivate?

They stay exactly as they are. You only stop inserting new templates on that site.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer. After checkout you see the payment details and a WhatsApp confirmation button.
